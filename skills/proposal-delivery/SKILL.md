---
name: proposal-delivery
description: Prepare the final user-facing text from the completed proposal as the publication node's action.
---

Read the final proposal at the location described in the incoming handoff and determine the required delivery scope.

For an initial proposal, prepare its full final text without wrapping the whole proposal in a fenced code block. For later user-requested modifications, prepare the updated sections with a concise prose explanation of which sections changed, what changed, and why. Do not show original-versus-modified blocks or use diff-like formatting.

Return `done` with the prepared delivery text as the natural-language body.
